# Battle for America 0.2.1 — Build 45

This release is based on the unreleased 0.2.0 candidate and the owner-corrected source baseline. It includes the carried candidate features below and the six difficulty/setup changes in this release. Build 45 is the first numbered output for this candidate.

## Difficulty and setup changes

- Moved difficulty and starting-map setup to the top of the new-campaign page, with difficulty shown before map controls.
- Added difficulty-specific starting national margins, player-favorable caps, starting funds and score multipliers:

  | Difficulty | Starting national margin | Favorable cap | Player funds at corruption 10 | Score multiplier |
  |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
  | Cupcake | +10 | none | $800m | 0.01× |
  | Easy | +4 | +7 | $300m | 0.5× |
  | Normal (saved ID `medium`) | 0 | +1 | $250m | 1× |
  | Hard | −4 | −3 | $250m | 2.5× |
  | Brutal | −8 | −8 | $250m | 7.5× |
  | Nightmare | −20 | −15 | $250m | 20× |

- Major-party margins mirror correctly for Democratic and Republican players. The slider stays −25 to +25 with neutral at zero; difficulty caps clamp only the player-favorable side of a new campaign.
- Existing corruption scaling remains for major-party funds. In Two Parties mode, third-party players and AI campaigns start with exactly $10m; other modes keep their existing third-party funding.
- State-by-state starting adjustments are available on Cupcake and Easy. Switching to Normal, Hard, Brutal, or Nightmare clears prior adjustments and disables the controls. Map randomness remains available. The engine enforces this restriction when creating preview, campaign and simulation games.
- Third-party national defaults, caps and slider bounds remain at their prior behavior. Their slider remains −15 to +25; the new centered range and caps apply to major-party setups.
- Realistic remains unchanged. Existing saves retain their saved environment, state leans, funds and difficulty. Existing career-score records remain unchanged; new results use the updated score table.

## Carried forward from the 0.2.0 candidate

These features were already present in the pinned candidate and are included unchanged in Build 45:

- Reduced new-campaign staff selection to three hires plus a separately assigned Chief while preserving confirmed legacy staff rosters and effects.
- Added 300 candidate-specific campaign event cards across 48 packs, candidate identity in event receipts, a week-12 guarantee queue, and saved decision recaps while retaining the regular event schedule.
- Added eligible candidate events to the developer picker and preserved the selected ticket and candidate through choice receipts.
- Added the requested Debs and Washington historical nominees with neutral defaults.
- Repaired the #049 debate-promise trigger and hardened receipt-dependent event selection against stale or altered sources.
- Carried the candidate's previously owner-approved Cook PVI values and legislature-class inputs unchanged. No district result-total or seat-count research or update was done for 0.2.1.
- Preserved Build 44 map/result presentation, early ballots, event-effect presentation, existing campaign saves and offline play.

## Owner correction applied

Fictional events keep their in-game triggers and do not require real-world legal filings, news reports or government records. This release adds no online data dependency. Historical official-data research items removed by the owner are excluded from 0.2.1 scope.
