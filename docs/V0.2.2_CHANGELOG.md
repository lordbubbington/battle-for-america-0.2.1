# Battle for America 0.2.2 — Regional Parties, Coattails & UI Stability

**Base:** `lordbubbington/battle-for-america-0.2.1` (`main` @ `1e51515261071a0ac594181c89710d657904b26d`, v0.2.1 / Build 45).
**Branch:** `v0.2.2` (unmerged).

## Summary of Changes (R01–R20)

### Regional Parties & Setup (R01–R10)
- **R01:** Removed Six Party Mode from new-game mode selection (`BUILD40_MODE_OPTIONS`, `SCENARIOS`) and blocked new `six-party` game creation while preserving legacy six-party save loading/resumption via `BUILD40_LEGACY_MODE_IDS`.
- **R02:** Locked Regional Parties difficulty to Normal (`medium`) with disabled difficulty and national-environment controls + explanations in setup, ignored national-environment contributions across regional campaign calculations (including news/events/coattails), and restored valid defaults when switching modes.
- **R03, R04, R10 & Owner Floor/Rebalance Rules:**
  - Unified all 10 regional parties, explicit targets, and frozen 10% border states (excluding Four Corners `AZ–CO` and `NM–UT`, no recursion, no AK/HI border additions) in `src/data/build39Scenarios.js` and `src/data/build40Elections.js`.
  - Implemented floor-honoring starting-share allocation in `src/lib/build40Elections.js`: strictly honors 5% major-party floors everywhere (except explicit MS/AL Southern Heritage Republican replacement), preserves third-party shares (`lib`/`grn`/`ind`), applies Utah priority override (`Deseret 61.7%`, `Rocky Mountain 10%`, `Dem 20%`, `Rep 5.1%`), trims Hawaii to fit 5% floors (`Hawaiian Ind. 82.0%`, `Deseret 4.8%`, `Dem 5%`, `Rep 5%`, `Lib+Grn 3.2%`), and flags conflicts only if >=10 pp from intended target (0 flagged).
  - Rebalanced opening major-party votes across eligible non-override states so Democrats and Republicans start even nationally (`26.81%` vs `26.81%`, diff `0.0000 pp`) when Regional Parties begins.
- **R05:** Added distinct base colors and derived light/mid/dark margin shade ladders for all 10 regional parties in `src/lib/marginColors.js`, updated `src/lib/polling.js`, `src/components/game/EvBar.jsx`, `src/components/game/night/Build40RaceDetails.jsx`, and `src/lib/downballot.js`.
- **R06:** Wired playable regional nominees (Amy Klobuchar, Gavin Newsom, Greg Abbott, Andy Beshear, Ron DeSantis, Evan McMullin) and 4 fictional AI-only nominees (Eleanor March / Boston Tea Party, Mara Kitt / Alaskan Independence, Leilani Kealoha / Hawaiian Independence, Jordan Mercer / Rocky Mountain); removed Conservation Party from new regional games; added the AK/HI flavor note; disabled Evan McMullin's independent Utah Senate candidacy and updated setup copy in new regional campaigns while preserving non-regional and legacy behavior.
- **R07:** Added regional elector pooling at `>= 270` combined regional EVs in `src/lib/presidency.js`, pooling behind the individual regional EV leader (tie-break: national popular vote, then stable regional party ID order), retaining original state EVs for map display, preserving contingent resolution at 269, and keeping popular-vote mode unoverridden.
- **R08:** Added regional-mode `×10` positive persuasion gain scaling (`scaleRegionalPersuasion` in `src/lib/v022Balance.js`) applied once at shared persuasion boundaries in `src/lib/gameEngine.js` (leaving turnout, starting shares, losses, and non-regional modes untouched).
- **R09:** Fixed national popular vote aggregation in `src/lib/polling.js` and `src/lib/presidency.js` to sum actual modeled state votes (deduplicated per state, never EV-weighted).

### Nick Bronze & South Carolina (R11–R13)
- **R11:** Updated `src/lib/useElectionForecast.js` and `src/components/game/PredictionMeter.jsx` so Nick Bronze refreshes only at the start of each week (holding the weekly snapshot across midweek actions and Election Night and persisting `nickBronzeSnapshot` across save/resume).
- **R12:** Added Nick Bronze half-weight turnout inputs (`baseline + 0.5 * (effective - baseline)`) in `src/lib/turnout.js` enabled exclusively by `src/lib/electionForecast.js`, leaving actual election counts at full strength.
- **R13:** Shifted South Carolina's base lean 3 points toward Republicans (`-14.15` → `-17.15`, `BASE_LEANS.SC = -17.1`) in `src/data/states.js`.

### Balance, Coattails & Screen Stability (R14–R20)
- **R15:** Added strictly-good event choice classification and player-only weighted draw for unscheduled player events in `src/lib/v022Balance.js` and `src/lib/gameEngine.js` (leaving AI and forced events untouched).
- **R16:** Added two-party-only 60/40 player-favorable directional news rebalancing in `src/lib/nationalNews.js` and `src/lib/worldNews.js` (preserving neutral/mixed stories, forced follow-ups, rival incidents, and non-two-party modes).
- **R14, R17, R18, R19:** Separated `baseStateGeneric` and `coattailByState` in `src/lib/environment.js`; blocked presidential-origin generic writes during week 1 (`applyRallyGeneric`, `opponentTurn` `genericDeltas`); implemented once-per-week gradual presidential coattails from week 2 onward (`0.20` coefficient, `±3.0` state cap, `0.25` weekly step, zero feedback loop, idempotent weekly receipt); removed overlapping presidential `marginOf` double-counting in `src/lib/downballot.js` for new campaigns while preserving legacy saves via save migration in `src/lib/gameEngine.js`.
- **R20:** Eliminated midweek forecast header collapse (`159.5px -> 56.25px`) by making `useElectionForecast` weekly-keyed with prior-week snapshot retention during weekly refreshes and reserving a responsive `min-h-[156px]` structural footprint in `PredictionMeter.jsx`.

### Two-Party Balance Patch (Post-R20 Calibration)
- **Normal Passive Income (`src/lib/gameEngine.js`, `incomeFor`):** Multiplied `(weeklyBase + levels * 0.55)` by `0.55` when `game?.difficultyId === 'medium'` to encourage fundraising/budgeting and narrow the Republican ending cash surplus.
- **Candidate Parity (`src/data/candidates.js`):** Tuned `burlison` (`nat: -0.5`, `region: { midwest: 1.5, south: 1, northeast: -2, west: -2 }`), `reagan` (`nat: 3.2`, `region: { west: 6, south: 4.5, midwest: 2.5, northeast: -4 }`), `youngkin` (`nat: 0.4`, `region: { south: 1.5, northeast: 1, midwest: -1, west: 0 }`), `haley` (`nat: 1.0`, `region: { south: 2, northeast: 0.5, midwest: -1, west: 0 }`), `desantis` (`nat: 0.3`, `region: { south: 3, midwest: 0.5, west: -1, northeast: -2 }`), `harris` (`nat: -2.2`), and `aoc` (`nat: -2.5`).
- **Senate Wave Responsiveness (`src/lib/downballot.js`):** Increased `RACE_WEIGHT.senate` from `0.68` to `0.85` and added a `-1.5` trim to `top` for `kind === 'senate'` when `coattailModelVersion >= 1` and `(Number(game.startingNationalGeneric) || 0) < 7` (preserving the D+7.3 benchmark).

### Two-Party 338-Matchup Playtest Follow-Up Fixes
- **Democratic Bench Traits (`src/data/candidateRatings.js`):** Subtly boosted `debate`, `speech`, and `rally` ratings across the Democratic bench (`ossoff`, `newsom`, `beshear`, `buttigieg`, `whitmer`, `shapiro`, `klobuchar`, `booker`, `warnock`, `moore`) while keeping `corruption` / fundraising asymmetry intact.
- **Candidate Parity (`src/data/candidates.js`):** Trimmed `abbott` (`south: 3.5`, `west: 1.0`) and `kemp` (`nat: 0.3`, `south: 3.0`), and lifted `booker` (`nat: 0.2`).
- **Scandal Spillover Scaling (`src/lib/gameEngine.js`, `applyChoice`):** Reduced all-state scandal vote-loss multiplier from `2.5` to `1.4` and added diminishing returns (`1 / (1 + 0.15 * priorScandals)`) for repeated player scandals.
- **Downballot Symmetry & House Neutral Baseline (`src/lib/downballot.js`):** Applied rival `aiFactions` downballot bonuses symmetrically alongside player `factionEffects(game).downballotBonus` (`netFactionDownballotBonus`), and added a `+1.5` neutral House competitiveness offset under `coattailModelVersion >= 1`.

- Decoupled `src/lib/gameEngine.js`, `src/lib/build40Field.js`, `src/lib/news.js`, `src/pages/Home.jsx`, `src/components/game/GameScreen.jsx`, `BoardPanel.jsx`, `EventCard.jsx`, `HistoryPanel.jsx`, and `ShiftPolicyPanel.jsx` so new campaigns start immediately playable with zero staff upkeep (`totalM: 0`), unmodified quality rolls, and legacy save compatibility.

### Consolidated Two-Party Balance Patch (1,650-Game Benchmark Follow-Up)
- **Democratic Field & Ratings Balancing (`src/data/candidates.js`, `src/data/candidateRatings.js`):** Updated national and regional modifiers across 11 Democratic candidates (`fdr`, `ossoff`, `newsom`, `aoc`, `harris`, `beshear`, `buttigieg`, `whitmer`, `booker`, `warnock`, `moore`), and boosted skill ratings for `fdr`, `buttigieg`, `whitmer`, and `moore` while leaving `corruption` unchanged.
- **Republican Bench & Historical Parity (`src/data/candidates.js`):** Rebalanced national, regional, and state modifiers for `reagan`, `desantis`, `abbott`, `timscott`, `youngkin`, and `kemp` to achieve two-party balance against the updated Democratic bench.
- **Medium Difficulty Scandal Pacing (`src/lib/gameEngine.js`):** Reduced medium difficulty `scandalScale` from `1` to `0.78` and added dampening (`scandalFreqDamp`) based on prior player scandals and recent-scandal occurrence in `applyBuild40Package`.
- **Neutral House Offset Calibration (`src/lib/downballot.js`):** Increased `houseNeutralOffset` from `1.5` to `3.5` in `houseDistrictMarginWithEffects` under `coattailModelVersion >= 1` when presidential district allocation is inactive.
